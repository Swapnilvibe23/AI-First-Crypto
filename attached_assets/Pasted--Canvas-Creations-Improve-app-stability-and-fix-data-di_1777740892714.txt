Planning OG meta tag implementation (10 seconds)
Given Instagram → web app → Beehiiv as your funnel, the priority order is clear:

1. OG Meta Tags — do this first, non-negotiable.
Instagram traffic means your link lives in your bio, stories, and DMs. When someone taps it and it previews as a blank card with no title or image, you lose the click before they even arrive. A proper preview with the app name, a sharp one-line description, and an image is the difference between "looks legit" and "looks like a spam link." This directly protects the top of your funnel.

2. Beehiiv newsletter sign-up prompt in the app — this is actually missing from the checklist but is your real priority.
You're driving Instagram traffic to the app to convert them to email subscribers. Right now the app has no path to Beehiiv at all. A non-intrusive sign-up strip — "Get the daily crypto snapshot in your inbox" with a Beehiiv embed or link — is the actual conversion mechanism. Without it, the funnel has no bottom.

3. PWA manifest — do after the above.
Once someone discovers you through Instagram and likes the app, "Add to Home Screen" turns a one-time visitor into a daily habit. Daily habit users are your best newsletter converts and your best organic sharers back to Instagram.
